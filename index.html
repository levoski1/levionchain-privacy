Privacy Policy & User Data Deletion

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 · Application: Levionchain Page Publisher

1. Overview

Levionchain Page Publisher is an internal content-publishing tool operated solely by the administrator of the Levionchain / Levicode Facebook Page to publish and schedule posts, images, videos, and Reels through the official Meta Graph API.

2. Information We Access

This application is not offered to the general public for third-party logins. It only accesses the Page management permissions explicitly authorized by the Page administrator (pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts, and pages_manage_metadata) to publish content and read Page publishing status.

3. How Information Is Used & Stored

  • Access tokens are stored locally in an encrypted/restricted environment file controlled solely by the Page owner.
  • No personal data of visitors, followers, or external Facebook users is collected, stored, sold, or shared with third parties.
  • All API communication happens directly with Meta's official Graph API endpoints over HTTPS.

4. User Data Deletion Instructions

Because this application does not collect or retain personal user data from external users, no third-party user records are stored on our servers. To revoke all Page permissions or request confirmation of data deletion at any time:

  1. Open your Facebook Account Settings & Privacy → Settings → Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites).
  2. Locate Levionchain and click Remove to immediately revoke all access tokens.
  3. You may also contact the Page administrator directly via our Facebook Page: facebook.com/115090074837929.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy occasionally to reflect operational or Meta platform requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
